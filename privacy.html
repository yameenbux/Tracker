Plumb

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

The short version: Plumb doesn’t collect, send or sell any of your data. There is no account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export it.

What Plumb stores

Your plan, weigh-ins, habit ticks, measurements, calorie numbers, session weights and progress photos. All of it is stored only on your device, inside Plumb’s private storage. Progress photos are kept inside the app and are never added to your photo library.

What Plumb sends

Nothing. Plumb has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It doesn’t need an internet connection to work.

Permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional) locks the app. The check is done by iOS; Plumb never sees your face or fingerprint data.
  • Camera and photos (optional) are used only when you add a progress photo. The photo is copied into Plumb’s private storage.
  • Notifications (optional) are used only for the daily weigh-in reminder, which is scheduled on your phone.

Backups and exports

When you tap Export, Plumb creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, email) is your choice, and it is then handled by that service. Photos are not included in backups.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes Plumb’s data in that backup, as it does for every app. This is controlled by your iPhone settings, not by Plumb.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything removes all of Plumb’s data and photos from your phone. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Children

Plumb isn’t directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
